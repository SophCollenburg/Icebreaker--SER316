Team Tueftelotter story submission -

There once was a little boy who loved to watch the world fly by as he played upon his beloved Drahtesel.

As much as this little boy loved his Drahtesel, nothing could compare to his love for Sauerkraut!

And so down he went, flying along the Hauptstrasse to search for his treat, wherever it may be!

He stopped at a Baeckerei to take in the sweet smell of the Apfelkuchen.

There he met a sweet and fluffy Hund, curled by the entryway.

The Hund perked up and trotted toward the Biergarten, where the smell of fresh Doener was drifting down the street.

"Away", cried the Gebaeck as the Hund crept closer, "You stay away from my pies!"

The Hund heaves a heavy sigh, dejected and hungry.  Its Hundemuede, sulking posture turning away.

The boy watched in disbelief as the Gebaeck grabbed its pies and jumped into his tiny Porsche, speeding away from the hungry Hund.

"I'll be back tomorrow!", the boy exclaimed, throwing his Dirndel on the ground in frustration.

Alas, as wettbewerbsfaehig as the boy was, he was even more hundemuede and knew he had to nap away his frustration!

As the boy retreated, he could hear Rammstein coming from the building.

Curious about the loud Musik, the boy turned around and walked toward the building to see what was happening inside.

His walk turned into a strut, his pace in rhythm with the beat.  His arms couldn't help but swing to the Ohrwurm.

He grabbed the window seal and peeked inside to see Rammstein himself cleaning the floor with a dirndel.

The boy offered him a Straubsauger, but he insisted that the dirndel had better rhythm.
